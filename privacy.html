BotBlack — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

BotBlack is an internal tool used by the owner of the «Чёрный» project to publish his own short videos to his own YouTube channel and TikTok account. It is not a public service and is used by one account only — the owner.

What data the app accesses

  • YouTube: uploading the owner's videos to his own channel, reading statistics and comments of his own videos and replying to them.
  • TikTok: basic profile info of the owner's account (display name, open ID) and uploading the owner's videos to his TikTok inbox as drafts (scopes user.info.basic, video.upload). The owner reviews and publishes each draft manually in the TikTok app.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to publish the owner's own videos and prepare internal statistics for the owner. Access tokens and video IDs are stored on the owner's private server. No data is sold, shared with or transferred to third parties, and no data is used for advertising.

Google API Services

BotBlack's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

TikTok

Use of TikTok data follows the TikTok Developer Terms of Service.

Revoking access

Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions. TikTok: Settings → Security → Apps and services permissions. After revocation stored tokens stop working and are deleted on request.

Contact

Via the Telegram bot @EZ5Signalsbot.

Политика конфиденциальности

BotBlack — внутренний инструмент автора проекта «Чёрный» для публикации собственных роликов на свой YouTube-канал и в свой TikTok. Пользуется только владелец.

Доступ: загрузка роликов владельца (YouTube — приватно; TikTok — в черновики, публикует владелец вручную), статистика и комментарии собственных роликов. Токены хранятся на закрытом сервере владельца. Данные не продаются, не передаются третьим лицам и не используются для рекламы.
